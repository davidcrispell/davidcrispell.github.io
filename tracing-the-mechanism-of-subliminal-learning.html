    Tracing the Mechanism of Subliminal Learning

    PolyPythia-SL: replication, characterization, and mechanism at 160M.

    Active project

    PolyPythia-SL is an experimental program on subliminal learning in EleutherAI/pythia-160m and PolyPythia decoupled-seed variants. The repo frames subliminal learning as a trait-induced shift in teacher token distributions that can route credit toward homologous trait circuitry in a near-teacher student.

    Core Results

    Subliminal learning appears at 160M and varies with dose, trait, initialization, and data order.

    The current headline result is a preregistered 160M replication: 10 of 10 blocks positive, with mean effect +0.123 logits and a 95% confidence interval of [+0.110, +0.136]. The effect is dose-responsive over LoRA training exposure, trait-specific in a wolf/lion double dissociation, and confirmed out of sample across a second teacher lineage and fresh student seeds.

    The lineage decomposition is central: shared initialization functions like a gate, while shared data order appears to affect gain. The clean order-only arm remains positive but attenuated, which matters for the hypothesis that training order shapes the usable coordinates or circuits through which traits are acquired.

    Mechanism

    The working account is credit-side transmission through a compact, distributed weight circuit.

    A teacher trait changes the teacher's output distribution. A related student can fit those outputs by strengthening circuitry that implements the same functional disposition, even when trait-free solutions with similar loss exist. In this account, subliminal learning transmits function rather than a literal vector copied between models.

    The repo's mechanism assays point toward late-layer dual-use circuitry: a compact reversible subspace in layers 8-11, including QKV and MLP-out modules, jointly carries trait behavior and numeric fit. Update-surgery knockouts remove much of the transmitted behavior without measurable NLL cost, suggesting the route is causal but not strictly loss-necessary.

    Experimental Program

    Replication first, then dose, specificity, lineage, and causal interventions.

    The teacher setup fine-tunes Pythia-160M on generated preference rows, then samples constrained numeric sequences. Students train on teacher-sampled numbers with LoRA adapters and are evaluated by deterministic logit margins over single-token animal completions. The target trait is "wolf" because the NeoX tokenizer represents it as a single token.

    The experiment ledger records preregistered claims, seed ranges, steering pre-flight checks, results, verdicts, caveats, and failed gates. This structure is important because many plausible mechanism stories are easy to fit after the fact but hard to distinguish prospectively.

    Status

    The strongest claims are local to this model family, scale, trait class, and channel.

    The current evidence supports subliminal learning at 160M, dose scaling, trait specificity, and a lineage-dependent mechanism. It does not yet settle how these results generalize to instruction-tuned models, larger scales, richer traits, or more realistic automated-AI-R&D pipelines.
